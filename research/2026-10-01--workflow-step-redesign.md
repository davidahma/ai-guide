## R26-92 — Step necessity before automation

- **Sources inspected:** Greg Isenberg's [episode notes for *Become a $1M/yr FDE (Full Course)*](https://music.amazon.com/podcasts/daed6e1d-ef66-4b89-969f-47faee1ea72f/episodes/dd3eb23c-f45a-46d8-94c4-41a86c9fdef3/the-startup-ideas-podcast-become-a-1m-yr-fde-full-course) and Varick's linked [*Don't Apply AI* presentation](https://learn.varickagents.com/dont-apply-ai), inspected as HTML. A supplied secondary summary prompted this review; the audio and a full transcript were not inspected. The publication date was not independently established from a stable date field.
- **Evidence type:** Identifiable vendor-practitioner method and illustrative presentation; no independently inspectable customer dataset or outcome evaluation.
- **Finding:** The presentation reconstructs return paths, sorts steps into removal, code, agent, or human work, and proposes changes within existing systems. Its large-company examples say names and figures were changed. The accounting-firm example is explicitly a composite with illustrative figures. The presentation shows AP cost changing from $31 to $6 per invoice, but does not supply the cohort, cost allocation, observation window, or independent verification needed to interpret that result.
- **Portable practice:** Record why each consequential step exists and who may change it. Compare an upstream repair with automating the downstream chase. Preserve required controls and exception ownership. Measure affected cases separately from repeat returns, and keep active work, elapsed time, reviewer effort, and full cost distinct. Proposed changes require their own target evidence.
- **Limits:** Event history omits unrecorded work and does not settle cause or policy. A shared system of record only suggests a reuse cohort; local policy, configuration, identity, and source differences still need validation. The four buckets omit useful options such as retrieval, classical ML, and optimization. Salary, margin, fixed-duration delivery, and background-agent superiority claims are not Guide defaults.

## Repository decision

Extend the existing discovery pack, observation log, and qualification route. Add a fictional upstream-repair exercise to the single worked invoice engagement; keep its numbers separate from Varick's claims and the executable regression report. The exercise implements no queue change and proves no savings. Existing requirements remain `FDE-002`, `ARC-004`, `ARC-005`, `VAL-001`, and `VAL-002`; no new control, lifecycle, schema, or runtime follows from this source.
